Detailed Description

Partition functions of RNA ensembles with unpaired sites.

of single RNA with up to two unpaired ranges i..j and k..j

Note
Sequence can be given and indexed 5'->3' (forward) or 3'->5' (reverse) direction. In the forward case, the sequence is indexed 1..length(seq) from 5'->3' as usual. In the latter case, the sequence positions start with 1 at the 3' end and run to length(seq) at the 5' end.
